## Shopping notes

- Structural material (PVC, rebar, cement, aggregates, tubular steel, lumber) → **local hardware + lumberyard**, not online.
- Consumables worth buying on Shopee: [trapal](https://da.gd/TvfwY) · [wood preservative](https://da.gd/ouTJOU) · [eye bolts](https://da.gd/6lV9I) · [turnbuckles](https://da.gd/qhdGi) · [welding rods](https://da.gd/lUZxCJ) · [deck screws](https://da.gd/l7Jj) · [nylon rope](https://da.gd/tn58xq) · [red oxide primer](https://da.gd/1BDhO)
- **Do not cut** the grade beam or the X-bracing to save money (~₱6,000 combined). They carry the whole safety case.
